PixelZone Gaming Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 15, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how PixelZone Gaming ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our gaming platform and related services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you interact with our services, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, password (hashed)
  • Profile Data: Avatar, game preferences, social media links (optional)
  • Payment Details: Encrypted payment information for premium services
  • Age Verification: Birth date for age-restricted content compliance

1.2 Automatic Data Collection

We automatically collect certain information when you use our services:

  • Gameplay Data: Match history, achievements, statistics
  • Device Information: Hardware specs, OS version, unique device identifiers
  • Network Data: IP address, connection speed, ping times
  • Usage Patterns: Feature usage, session duration, clickstream data

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: To provide and maintain our gaming services
  • Personalization: To customize your gaming experience and recommendations
  • Security: To detect and prevent cheating, fraud, and unauthorized access
  • Communications: To send service updates, security alerts, and support responses
  • Analytics: To improve game balance, matchmaking, and service performance
  • Legal Compliance: To meet regulatory requirements and enforce our Terms of Service

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information in these limited circumstances:

3.1 Service Providers

With trusted partners who assist in:

  • Payment processing (Stripe, PayPal)
  • Cloud hosting services (AWS, Google Cloud)
  • Customer support platforms
  • Analytics providers (Google Analytics, GameAnalytics)

3.2 Legal Requirements

When required by law or to protect our rights:

  • In response to valid legal process (subpoenas, court orders)
  • To investigate potential violations of our policies
  • To prevent harm to our users or the public

3.3 Business Transfers

In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales, where user data may be transferred as part of the business assets.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use various technologies to enhance your experience:

Type Purpose How to Control
Essential Cookies Session maintenance, security Required for service operation

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our services are not directed to children under 13. For users under 13:

  • We require verifiable parental consent for data collection
  • We limit data collection to only what's necessary
  • Parents can review or delete their child's information

For EU users, we comply with GDPR age requirements (16+ or local age of consent).

6. Data Security Measures

We implement robust security protections:

  • Encryption: AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit
  • Access Controls: Role-based access with multi-factor authentication
  • Regular Audits: Annual penetration testing and security assessments
  • Incident Response: 24/7 monitoring and breach notification procedures

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have these rights:

Access

Request a copy of your personal data

Correction

Update inaccurate information

Deletion

Request erasure under certain conditions

Restriction

Limit how we use your data

Portability

Receive your data in a machine-readable format

Objection

Opt-out of certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@pixelzone.com.

8. International Data Transfers

As a global service, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your residence. We ensure adequate protections through:

  • EU Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Privacy Shield certification (where applicable)
  • Binding Corporate Rules for intra-company transfers

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically. We'll notify you of material changes through:

  • In-service notifications
  • Email alerts (for account holders)
  • Updated "Last Revised" date at the top of this policy

Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

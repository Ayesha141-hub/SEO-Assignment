Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 15, 2025

At PixelZone Gaming ("we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our gaming platform and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you register an account, we may collect:

  • Name and username
  • Email address
  • Age verification (for age-restricted content)
  • Payment information (for premium services)

1.2 Gameplay Data

We automatically collect:

  • Game progress and achievements
  • Multiplayer match history
  • Device information (hardware specs, OS version)
  • IP address and approximate location

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our gaming services
  • To personalize your gaming experience
  • To detect and prevent cheating/fraud
  • To send service-related communications
  • For analytics and service optimization

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Game developers for multiplayer functionality
  • Payment processors for transactions
  • Legal authorities when required by law

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to:

  • Maintain login sessions
  • Remember preferences
  • Analyze traffic patterns

You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We comply with COPPA and do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard measures including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls

7. Your Rights

You may:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent (where applicable)
